# ReCord+ Privacy Policy

Effective on first release (October 2026) · Publisher: DevCrest (Michael Wright), devcrest.support@gmail.com, 37 Old Main Road, Hillcrest, KZN, South Africa

**Short version: ReCord+ does not collect, send, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no internet access.**

## What the App does with data
- **Audio.** The App records the audio channels you select from a USB audio interface you connect to your device. The recordings are saved as files on your device only. We cannot see them and they are never uploaded. You decide whether to share, copy or delete them.
- **Stored on your device only.** Your preferences (theme, sample rate, file format, split and silence settings), the track labels you type and your track selection are saved in the App's private storage. Deleting the App deletes them. Recordings are saved in the Recordings/ReCord+ folder (Android; some formats in the App's own folder) or in the App's folder visible in the Files app (iOS), and you can keep or remove them.
- **No internet.** The App does not connect to the internet and has no servers of ours behind it. (On Android the App does not request the Internet permission.)
- **Microphone permission.** Android and iOS call access to USB audio inputs "microphone" access. The App uses it only to read the USB audio interface you connect, only while it is open, and only records when you tap Record. It does not use the device's built-in microphone.
- **Notifications (Android).** A notification is shown while a recording runs, so recording continues when the screen is off. It contains no personal data.
- **Children.** The App is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Stores
Apple and Google process your purchase and may collect data under their own policies. We receive only aggregated sales and crash information from them, not your identity.

## Your rights
Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your address only to reply and delete it on request. For EU/UK users, GDPR/UK GDPR (legitimate interest in answering support requests); for South African users, POPIA.

## Changes and contact
If this ever changes we will update this page and the store privacy declarations before the App does anything new. Questions: devcrest.support@gmail.com.
